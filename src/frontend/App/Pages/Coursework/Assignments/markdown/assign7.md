#### Asignment 7: Define the following Agile roles: Product Manager, Scrum Master, UX Designer, Developer/Engineer, QA/SDET, and DevOps.

<ul>
                    <li>
                        <strong>Product Manager (PM)</strong> — Owns the product
                        vision and decides what should be built and why. Works
                        with stakeholders and prioritizes the product backlog.
                    </li>
                    <li>
                        <strong>Scrum Master</strong> — Facilitates Agile
                        ceremonies, removes blockers, and ensures the team
                        follows Scrum processes effectively.
                    </li>
                    <li>
                        <strong>UX Designer</strong> — Focuses on user research,
                        wireframes, prototypes, and creating intuitive,
                        user-centered experiences.
                    </li>
                    <li>
                        <strong>Developer / Engineer</strong> — Implements
                        features by writing code, fixing bugs, and collaborating
                        closely with PM, UX, QA, and DevOps.
                    </li>
                    <li>
                        <strong>QA / SDET</strong> — Ensures product quality
                        through testing, creating test plans, writing automated
                        tests, and validating features.
                    </li>
                    <li>
                        <strong>DevOps</strong> — Manages infrastructure, CI/CD
                        pipelines, deployments, and automations to ensure stable
                        and reliable releases.
                    </li>
                </ul>
